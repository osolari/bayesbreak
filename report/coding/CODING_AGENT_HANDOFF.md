## Author-fixed scientific scope

BayesBreak is the method developed in Generalized Hierarchical Bayesian Segmentation with Irregular Designs, Multi-Sequence Hierarchies, and Grouped/Latent-Group Designs. For supported observation models, family-specific segment marginal likelihoods and posterior moments enter common sum-product recursions for posterior quantities and a separate max-sum recursion with backtracking for the joint MAP partition. The implementation must preserve irregular-design priors, common and group-specific changepoints across multiple sequences, known groups, and the finite latent-group criterion stated in the manuscript.

- Do not change the manuscript title or replace generalized hierarchical Bayesian segmentation as the main scientific narrative without explicit author approval.
- Do not overwrite populated archived numerical results; a corrected computation receives a new result ID and an explicit parent-result link.
- Do not interpret the finite latent-group criterion as a normalized finite-mixture likelihood.
- Do not use marginal changepoint modes as the joint MAP partition.

## Failure states

### FAIL-BB-001

**Condition:** Nonfinite or dimensionally inconsistent segment scores.

**Required behavior:** Stop before partition inference and emit the offending family, segment coordinates, and configuration hash.

### FAIL-BB-002

**Condition:** Comparator prediction and reference axes differ.

**Required behavior:** Reject metric computation; preserve the attempted run only as a diagnostic record.

### FAIL-BB-003

**Condition:** Posterior prediction requested for an unsupported family or coordinate outside support.

**Required behavior:** Raise an explicit error unless a named, recorded extrapolation rule is selected.

### FAIL-BB-004

**Condition:** Latent-group objective is nonmonotone beyond tolerance or returned objective differs from the final trace value.

**Required behavior:** Mark the restart invalid and do not select it as the optimum.

### FAIL-BB-005

**Condition:** A required test exceeds its profiled bound.

**Required behavior:** Report unresolved with owner, environment, seed, and profile; do not count it as a pass.

### FAIL-BB-006

**Condition:** A corrected result lacks a parent link or required hashes.

**Required behavior:** Reject release of the result and retain the archived parent unchanged.
