\section{Recommended reading sequence}
A reader focused on the BayesBreak statistical formulation should begin with product partitions and exact
Bayesian recursion (Barry--Hartigan, Fearnhead, Hutter), then read design-aware partitioning
(Scargle, PPMx, generalized PPM), multiple-sequence work (BASIC and JRPM), and finally the
block-local approximation sources. The clustering extension should be read beside Corradin et al.;
the finite latent-group criterion in BayesBreak is intentionally narrower than a normalized latent-mixture model.
