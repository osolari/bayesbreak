# Technical narrative boundary

The technical route is about the statistical model, exact and approximate posterior computation, hierarchical extensions, optimization, numerical properties, empirical behavior, and limitations.

It may compare BayesBreak with product-partition models, exact Bayesian changepoint recursions, hierarchical multi-sequence methods, latent-allocation methods, and nonconjugate integration techniques when the comparison is precise and cited.

It may not:

- change the title or principal contribution;
- claim universal applicability beyond the segment-marginal-likelihood and factorization conditions;
- present the finite latent-group criterion as a normalized mixture likelihood;
- present descriptive case studies as external-accuracy evaluations;
- omit excluded calculations or unresolved test states when discussing software reliability;
- introduce implementation, publication, staffing, or decision roadmaps;
- replace established terminology with vague branding.
