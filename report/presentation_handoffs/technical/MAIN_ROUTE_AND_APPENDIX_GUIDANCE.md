# Technical main-route and appendix guidance

## Main route candidates

- Problem formulation and inferential targets.
- Segment marginal likelihoods for exponential-family models.
- Sum-product versus max-sum dynamic programming.
- Irregular-design prior factors.
- Shared-boundary and finite latent-group extensions.
- One principal exact theorem and the conditional approximation theorem.
- Selected calibration, latent-group, timing, and application figures.
- Limitations and failure boundaries.

## Appendix candidates

- Family-specific conjugate formulas and moment conditions.
- Full forward/backward identities.
- Joint MAP proof details and deterministic tie handling.
- Finite-candidate concentration assumptions and proof structure.
- Latent-group minorization derivation.
- Approximation-error inequalities.
- Metric matching definition and `NA` matched-MAE rule.
- Full real-result interpretation ledger and test status.

The later presentation workflow may choose duration-specific routes but should not remove assumptions needed to interpret a theorem or result.
