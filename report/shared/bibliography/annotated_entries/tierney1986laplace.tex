\paragraph{\texttt{tierney1986laplace}: \citet{tierney1986laplace}.}
Tierney and Kadane develop Laplace approximations for posterior moments and marginal posterior densities under regularity conditions that include a suitably regular, unimodal likelihood-times-prior integrand. BayesBreak cites this paper as a foundation for Laplace evaluation of a nonconjugate segment marginal likelihood. The source does not imply a uniform error bound over all candidate segments, nor does it establish a BayesBreak-specific convergence rate. Any use in BayesBreak therefore requires the segment objective, mode, curvature, and approximation error to be assessed over the admissible segment set; the partition-level error statements apply only after such a segment-level bound has been established. \textit{Verification status: publisher metadata and article scope checked for the corrective revision.}
