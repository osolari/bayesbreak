
\paragraph{\texttt{green1995rjMCMC}: \citet{green1995rjMCMC}.}
Green introduces reversible-jump Markov chain Monte Carlo for Bayesian model determination across spaces of differing dimension. Changepoint models can use this machinery to sample segment count and boundaries without the finite factorization required by exact dynamic programming. BayesBreak cites the work as the principal trans-dimensional Bayesian alternative and uses it to state its own restriction: exactness comes from finite ordered support and local factorization, not from universal Bayesian computation. \textit{Verification status: metadata retained; foundational computational role.}
