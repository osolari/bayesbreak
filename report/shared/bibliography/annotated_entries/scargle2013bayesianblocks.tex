
\paragraph{\texttt{scargle2013bayesianblocks}: \citet{scargle2013bayesianblocks}.}
Scargle and coauthors develop Bayesian Blocks for optimal representations under arbitrary sampling modes, gaps, exposures, and several data modes. This is important prior art for irregularly sampled segmentation. BayesBreak therefore narrows its claim: likelihood exposure, segment cohesion, and boundary hazard are distinct modeling components, and ordered cohesion-plus-hazard factors can be consumed by the same DP. The source is closest work for irregular-design handling, not support for the earlier conflated Poisson-gap derivation. \textit{Verification status: primary source checked in Phase 1.}
