\paragraph{\texttt{tibshirani2005fused}: \citet{tibshirani2005fused}.}
Tibshirani and coauthors formulate the fused lasso, combining sparsity and adjacent-difference penalties to estimate piecewise-constant structure. It is a natural point-estimation baseline for offline segmentation. BayesBreak differs through probabilistic integration over segment parameters and posterior inference over changepoint configurations; a fused-lasso tuning path is not equivalent to a prior on the number of segments. The source informs baseline selection and misspecification analysis. \textit{Verification status: journal title, volume, pages, and DOI independently checked against the publisher record in Phase~6.}
