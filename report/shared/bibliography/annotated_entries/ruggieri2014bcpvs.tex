
\paragraph{\texttt{ruggieri2014bcpvs}: \citet{ruggieri2014bcpvs}.}
Ruggieri and Lawrence combine Bayesian changepoint inference with variable selection and apply the method to a paleoclimate proxy record. The work demonstrates that scientifically meaningful segmentation often couples regime changes with additional structural choices.
