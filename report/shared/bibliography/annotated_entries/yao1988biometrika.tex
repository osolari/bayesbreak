\paragraph{\texttt{yao1988biometrika}: \citet{yao1988biometrika}.}
Yao studies estimation of the number of changepoints in an independent normal sequence using Schwarz's criterion and establishes weak consistency of the proposed estimator. This paper represents information-criterion selection of segment count. BayesBreak uses it as a contrast to assigning a prior to the number of segments, integrating over that number, and reporting its posterior distribution together with posterior changepoint summaries and a separate joint MAP partition. These procedures address related but nonidentical inferential targets. \textit{Verification status: author name, title, journal, volume, pages, DOI, and stated result independently checked against the publisher record in Phase~6.}
