
\paragraph{\texttt{fearnhead2006exact}: \citet{fearnhead2006exact}.}
Fearnhead gives exact and efficient Bayesian inference for a class of multiple-changepoint models with unknown changepoint count, conditionally independent segments, product-partition structure, and quadratic exact computation. This is the closest foundational work for BayesBreak's sum-product recursion and materially constrains novelty. BayesBreak's contribution is the generalized exponential-family segment formulation, posterior moments and changepoint summaries computed by common recursions, the separate max-sum calculation, irregular-design and multiple-sequence extensions, and the finite latent-group criterion. \textit{Verification status: central primary source checked in Phase 1.}
