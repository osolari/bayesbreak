
\paragraph{\texttt{frick2014smuce}: \citet{frick2014smuce}.}
Frick, Munk, and Sieling develop multiscale changepoint inference with simultaneous error control and confidence statements. SMUCE is a relevant baseline because it emphasizes inferential reliability rather than only point recovery. BayesBreak uses it to design task-matched comparisons for boundary recovery and uncertainty, while recognizing that its posterior probabilities and Bayes curves are not directly equivalent to multiscale confidence sets. Any benchmark must therefore specify the inferential target and calibration criterion. \textit{Verification status: publisher metadata independently checked and corrected in Phase~6; baseline role retained.}
