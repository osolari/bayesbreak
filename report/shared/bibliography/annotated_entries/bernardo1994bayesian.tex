
\paragraph{\texttt{bernardo1994bayesian}: \citet{bernardo1994bayesian}.}
Bernardo and Smith provide the general Bayesian foundations used to interpret marginal likelihoods, posterior model probabilities, posterior predictive quantities, and decision-relevant uncertainty. In BayesBreak, the book supports the distinction between evidence, posterior summaries, and exported point decisions. It does not supply a changepoint algorithm or validate any archived numerical result.
