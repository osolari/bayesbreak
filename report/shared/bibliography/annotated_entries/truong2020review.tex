
\paragraph{\texttt{truong2020review}: \citet{truong2020review}.}
Truong, Oudre, and Vayatis provide a selective review and taxonomy of offline changepoint methods, including costs, search methods, and evaluation considerations. BayesBreak uses the review to organize baseline families and to prevent category errors between detection, segmentation, online monitoring, and posterior inference. It is a secondary synthesis rather than evidence for a BayesBreak theorem or result. \textit{Verification status: central review source checked in Phase 1.}
