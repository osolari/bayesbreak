
\paragraph{\texttt{quinlan2024jrpm}: \citet{quinlan2024jrpm}.}
Quinlan, Page, and Castro develop joint random partition models that permit dependence among multiple partitions. This is richer than BayesBreak's exact common-boundary specialization. The source is used to state the tradeoff precisely: BayesBreak gains a simple exact DP by imposing a shared partition (or known groups), while JRPM targets flexible dependence across series. Broad claims about general multivariate partition inference are therefore excluded. \textit{Verification status: central primary source checked in Phase 1.}
