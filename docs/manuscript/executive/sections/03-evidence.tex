\subsection{Selected executed simulation results}

\begin{resultbox}[Real executed numerical results]
In the archived studies, the repeated Gaussian boundary-calibration experiment produced ECE approximately $0.010$ and Brier score approximately $0.011$. A two-group latent-allocation experiment produced $96\%$ hard assignment accuracy at $\sigma=1.0$ with 24 sequences. The $k_{\max}=20$ timing study increased from $0.0455$ seconds at $n=50$ to $1.0585$ seconds at $n=800$. These are results for their stated designs, not universal performance guarantees.
\end{resultbox}

\subsection{Applied studies}

Four archived applications illustrate well-log, array-CGH, equity-volatility, and methylation analyses. They show that the implementation can fit heterogeneous ordered datasets using the corresponding segment models and hierarchical structures. They are descriptive case studies because independent reference changepoints are unavailable. Fitted vertical markers are MAP changepoints, not externally established geological, genomic, or macroeconomic events.
