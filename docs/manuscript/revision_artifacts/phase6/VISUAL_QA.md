# Phase 6 visual QA

All four compiled documents were rendered at 150 dpi and inspected page by page through 22 contact sheets. The inspection covered 168 technical-book pages, 35 two-column paper pages, 42 single-column paper pages, and 12 executive-summary pages, for 257 pages in total.

Targeted full-page checks were also performed on technical-book pages 31, 43, 56, 62, and 71, which contain the factorized prior, the sum-product theorem and proof, the Poisson interval-occupancy calculation, the latent-group Jensen objective, and the nonconjugate perturbation result.

No blank pages, clipped text or equations, overlapping elements, broken glyphs, missing figure frames, unreadable tables, or unresolved reference markers were observed.
