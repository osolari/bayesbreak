
\paragraph{\texttt{barry1992ppm}: \citet{barry1992ppm}.}
Barry and Hartigan introduce product partition models for changepoint problems, including the cohesion factorization that underlies segment-local prior contributions. This is direct prior art for BayesBreak's factorized partition prior. BayesBreak narrows the setting to ordered contiguous partitions and makes the local prior factorization explicit by separating segment cohesion $c_x(i,j)$ from an interior-boundary hazard $h_x(j)$, both of which preserve dynamic-programming factorization. The source prevents any claim that cohesion priors originate here. \textit{Verification status: central primary source checked in Phase 1.}
