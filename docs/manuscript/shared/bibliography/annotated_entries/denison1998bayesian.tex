\paragraph{\texttt{denison1998bayesian}: \citet{denison1998bayesian}.}
Denison, Mallick, and Smith develop automatic Bayesian curve fitting with a joint model for the number and positions of knots in piecewise-polynomial representations and reversible-jump Markov chain Monte Carlo for posterior computation. BayesBreak uses the paper as a contrasting Bayesian model-space and computational lineage rather than as an exact changepoint dynamic-programming reference. The comparison makes BayesBreak's scope precise: its exact recursions require a finite ordered candidate set and segment-factorized marginal likelihoods, and do not subsume arbitrary trans-dimensional curve models. \textit{Verification status: title, journal, volume, pages, DOI, and methodological description independently checked against the Royal Statistical Society publisher record in Phase~6; the previously supplied JASA changepoint citation was incorrect.}
