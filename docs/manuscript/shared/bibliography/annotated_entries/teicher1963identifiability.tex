\paragraph{\texttt{teicher1963identifiability}: \citet{teicher1963identifiability}.}
Teicher establishes classical identifiability results for finite mixtures under conditions on normalized component distributions. The reference is retained to mark a boundary of applicability. BayesBreak's current subject--template scores are positive partition scores rather than normalized subject-level component densities, so finite-mixture identifiability does not follow from Teicher's result. The book limits the established latent-template theory to monotonicity of the stated objective and records label permutations, duplicate templates, and collapsed solutions as failure modes. \textit{Verification status: journal metadata and DOI independently checked against the Project Euclid record in Phase~6; earlier overextension of the identifiability result remains removed.}
