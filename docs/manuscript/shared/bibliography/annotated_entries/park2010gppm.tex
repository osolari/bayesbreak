
\paragraph{\texttt{park2010gppm}: \citet{park2010gppm}.}
Park and Dunson develop generalized product partition models, broadening the ways local partition factors can encode prior structure. BayesBreak uses the work to situate its ordered prior specification: only factors that can be assigned locally to admissible contiguous blocks and interior endpoints can enter the unchanged finite DP. The book's result is therefore a transparent specialization and implementation rule, not a general invention of product-partition priors. \textit{Verification status: primary source checked in Phase 1.}
