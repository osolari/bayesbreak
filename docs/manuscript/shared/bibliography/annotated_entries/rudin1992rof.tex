\paragraph{\texttt{rudin1992rof}: \citet{rudin1992rof}.}
Rudin, Osher, and Fatemi introduce total-variation regularization for edge-preserving reconstruction. The work is a mathematical ancestor of piecewise-constant penalization, not a Bayesian changepoint posterior method. BayesBreak uses this lineage to contextualize fused and total-variation baselines and to distinguish a penalized point estimator from a posterior distribution over partitions. \textit{Verification status: bibliographic metadata and DOI independently checked against the publisher record in Phase~6; relationship is background only.}
