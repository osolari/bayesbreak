
\paragraph{\texttt{barry1993bayesCP}: \citet{barry1993bayesCP}.}
Barry and Hartigan develop a Bayesian analysis for changepoint problems using the product-partition framework. BayesBreak cites this paper for the Bayesian changepoint and cohesion lineage, including posterior uncertainty about partitions. The exact BayesBreak delta is not generic Bayesian changepoint inference; it is the generalized exponential-family segment formulation, the collection of posterior quantities computed by the sum-product recursion, the hierarchical extensions, and the separately computed joint MAP partition. The comparison also motivates calibrated novelty language. \textit{Verification status: central primary source checked in Phase 1.}
