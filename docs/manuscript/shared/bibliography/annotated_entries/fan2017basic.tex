
\paragraph{\texttt{fan2017basic}: \citet{fan2017basic}.}
Fan and Mackey's BASIC model addresses simultaneous changepoints across many sequences and includes copy-number and S\&P volatility applications. It is the closest multisequence comparator and overlaps the domains of two BayesBreak case studies. BayesBreak's narrower exact result assumes one common boundary vector and conditionally independent subject contributions whose integrated block evidences multiply. The revised book presents this as a restricted exact pooling regime, not the first Bayesian multisequence changepoint method. \textit{Verification status: central primary source checked in Phase 1.}
