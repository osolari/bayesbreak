
\paragraph{\texttt{punskaya2002bayesian}: \citet{punskaya2002bayesian}.}
Punskaya and coauthors develop Bayesian curve fitting by MCMC with signal-segmentation applications. The paper supplies a sampling-based Bayesian alternative that can accommodate models outside BayesBreak's exact factorized regime. BayesBreak uses it to motivate posterior uncertainty while explaining why its own exact recursions require segment-local integrated scores. It is not used to justify the archived BayesBreak numerical values. \textit{Verification status: canonical IEEE journal metadata retained in the supplied bibliography.}
