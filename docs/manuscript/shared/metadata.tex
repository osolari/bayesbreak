\newcommand{\ProjectDescription}{%
A technical treatment of generalized hierarchical Bayesian segmentation for ordered data.
Segment marginal likelihoods from conjugate exponential-family models are combined with
exact dynamic programming over contiguous partitions, with extensions for irregular designs,
shared and group-specific changepoints, latent groups, and explicitly approximate
nonconjugate segment models.}
