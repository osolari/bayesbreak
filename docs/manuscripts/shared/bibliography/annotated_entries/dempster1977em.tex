\paragraph{\texttt{dempster1977em}: \citet{dempster1977em}.}
Dempster, Laird, and Rubin establish the expectation--maximization algorithm for maximum-likelihood estimation with incomplete data. The BayesBreak latent-template routine uses a responsibility-weighted alternating optimization, but its subject--template quantities are prior-weighted partition scores rather than normalized component densities. The book therefore cites EM as optimization background and describes the routine through its displayed finite objective and Jensen minorization; it does not claim an observed-data finite-mixture likelihood or import identifiability conclusions from mixture theory.
