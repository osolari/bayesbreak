    Results and current evidence scope¶

    All E00–E10 confirmatory experiments have been executed and passed their execution gate (gate_pass=True). E00–E08 and E10 are scientifically complete. E09 (real-data analysis) passed its execution gate but has incomplete scientific evaluation; the limitations are documented explicitly below.

    Completed figures and tables are the outputs of specific models and datasets under the stated priors and configurations. They are not universal validation of the framework.

    E00: Exactness checks¶

    Exhaustive partition enumeration against analytic reference values. Four exponential families (Gaussian, Poisson, Binomial, NegBin), sequence lengths \(n \in \{2,\ldots,10\}\), nonuniform prior factors, and moments.

    380 oracle checks — all 380 passed (artifact 03a4d1c38bfe08c1).

    Check category Max error Tolerance
    Partition sums and normalizers \(1.07 \times 10^{-14}\) \(10^{-8}\) (log scale)
    Boundary probabilities and first moments \(7.11 \times 10^{-15}\) \(10^{-9}\)
    Second-moment Bayes curves (Gaussian) \(6.30 \times 10^{-7}\) \(10^{-6}\)
    Integrated likelihoods vs. 1-D adaptive quadrature \(3.91 \times 10^{-14}\) \(10^{-6}\) (absolute)

    E01–E02: Gaussian recovery and cross-family portability¶

    ECE 0.00044–0.0081, Brier ≤ 0.020, F1@3 > 0.93 at Δ/σ = 2 (E01). F1@3 0.88–1.00 at strong effect across Gaussian, Poisson, Binomial, NegBin (E02). Calibration target: conditional boundary-event marginals P(b_i = 1 | y, k_map).

    E03–E05: Irregular designs, shared-boundary pooling, latent templates¶

    E03 verifies that gap-aware partition priors correctly down-weight short candidate segments on irregular grids. E04 confirms exact shared-boundary posterior pooling for multi-subject sequences. E05 validates the finite-template EM objective and restart-ranking rule.

    E06: Nonconjugate approximation accuracy¶

    Comparison of Laplace, Jaakkola–Jordan, Pólya–Gamma mean field (PG-VB), expectation propagation, and 1-D Gauss–Hermite quadrature against analytic Gaussian reference scores (1800 blocks).

    Key finding: pg_vb and jj invoke the same JJ numerical routine — their results are identical by construction. Laplace: median 0.021, max 0.038. JJ: median 0.222, max 0.977. EP: median 3.779, max 30.001. JJ has zero lower-bound violations. EP does not satisfy the uniform error bound required for Theorem 1 in the block-error regime sampled.

    E07: Model misspecification¶

    Misspecification grid confirming that family mismatches degrade calibration in interpretable ways. Findings document family-specific failure modes rather than a general robustness claim.

    E08: Posterior prediction¶

    Held-out log-likelihood evaluation confirming that the posterior-predictive layer correctly dispatches to family-specific support rules. PIT uniformity verified for conjugate families with closed-form CDFs.

    E09: Real-data analysis (execution complete; scientific completeness incomplete)¶

    The E09 protocol defines 31 cells (dataset × prior × \(k_{\max}\) variant combinations). Six cells were resolved; 25 were truncated at their declared \(k_{\max}\) and the unrestricted MAP segment counts for those cells are unknown. The eight rows in Table R (real-data summary) are the one per dataset–prior combination that reached a final sweep; the full 31-cell protocol is recorded in artifact 0f5281c46ee8976e.

    Dataset Result Scientific status
    Well-log NMR (n=4050) \(\widehat{k}=23\) Resolved
    Array-CGH single (n=2215) \(\widehat{k}=71\) Resolved
    Array-CGH pooled (S=43) Truncated at \(k_{\max}=60\) Unresolved
    CpG methylation (n=1904) Truncated at \(k_{\max}=120\) (both count priors) Unresolved
    S&P 500 volatility Excluded from authentic real-data experiment Simulated analog only

    CGH precision model. The single-subject CGH analysis uses per-probe precision weights derived from rolling median absolute deviation (MAD) of log₂ ratio differences, treating the resulting relative inverse-variance values as known observation precisions. These weights are data-derived rather than physically calibrated: the rolling-MAD specification is a heuristic choice, and inference under a uniform-precision or platform-calibrated noise model has not been compared.

    Methylation truncation. Both methylation cells were truncated at \(k_{\max}=120\); sensitivity of the MAP segment count to \(k_{\max}\) was not characterized for that dataset.

    Baselines. R-based comparators (CBS, SMUCE) are unavailable in this environment. Python-based comparators via ruptures are available but the planned cross-comparator study (Table P4) has not been executed.

    E10: Computational scaling¶

    Memory and timing measurements at \(n \in \{128, 256, 512, 1024, 2048\}\). Peak memory ≈ 500 MB at \(n=2048\); log-log slope 1.94–2.00 consistent with \(\mathcal{O}(n^2)\) dominated by the block-evidence matrix. Forward-backward slope 1.10–1.24 in practice. End-to-end 3.25 s at \(n=2048\), \(k_{\max}=20\). Artifact bece422c9d90093f.

    Outstanding limitations¶

    The following limitations apply to the current experimental evidence:

    • E09 CGH pooled and methylation MAP counts are unknown (truncated).
    • R-based comparator study (CBS, SMUCE) has not been executed.
    • Sensitivity of methylation segmentation to \(k_{\max}\) was not characterized.
    • The rolling-MAD CGH precision model is a heuristic; no comparison against uniform-precision or calibrated alternatives has been performed.
    • SPX (S&P 500) was excluded from the authentic real-data experiment; reported SPX figures use a simulated GARCH-like analog.
    • External changepoint annotations are unavailable for all four datasets; reported MAP boundaries are not validated against independent truth.

    These limitations are stated in full in the Limitations section of the paper (docs/manuscripts/paper/sections/10-limitations.tex).
